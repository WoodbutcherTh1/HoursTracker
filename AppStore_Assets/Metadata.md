HoursTracker is the fastest way to log your work hours and know exactly what you'll earn — this shift, this week, and this month.

• Night shifts and rest‑day premiums handled automatically.
• Manage multiple workplaces, each with its own pay rules.
• Export clean PDF reports for payday.
• Home‑screen widgets and a Lock Screen Live Activity for the running shift.
• Apple Watch companion.

BUILT FOR EVERY LANGUAGE
Full right‑to‑left (RTL) support for Hebrew and Arabic — the entire interface, including the weekly graph, mirrors correctly so the week reads naturally right‑to‑left. Also available in English, Russian and Thai.

FAST AND PRIVATE
Your data stays on your device by default, with optional private iCloud sync. The app is engineered to be instant — no spinners, no lag.

Track your hours. Know your pay. Own your month.
