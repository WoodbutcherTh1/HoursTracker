### English

**HoursTracker — hours and pay tracking built for Israeli labor law**

An app built specifically for workers in Israel: it tracks your work hours, calculates your pay the way the law requires — including 125%/150% overtime tiers, travel allowance, and tax credit points — and helps you understand exactly what you should be earning.

**Why HoursTracker?**

⏱️ One-tap clock in/out, with reminders if you forget
💵 Automatic pay calculation under the Hours of Work and Rest Law — regular hours, 125%, 150%, and travel allowance
🧾 A "pay breakdown" view showing exactly what comes out for income tax, National Insurance, and health tax — based on your credit points, marital status, and age
📸 Smart payslip scanner — photograph your payslip and let the app pull out the numbers automatically
📚 A tidy payslip library you can sort by date or amount
📊 A History tab with weekly and monthly views, and a net/gross summary
🎨 A Home screen you can make your own — colors, stat cards, all wrapped in a premium dark design
🔒 Works fully offline; your data syncs across your devices via iCloud
🌍 Available in Hebrew, Arabic, and English

All tax figures are planning estimates only and are not official tax or payroll advice.
