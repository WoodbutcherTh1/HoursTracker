### English

**HoursTracker — hours and pay tracking built for Israeli labor law**

An app made for workers in Israel: it tracks your hours, calculates your pay the way the law requires — 125%/150% overtime, rest days and holidays, travel allowance and tax credit points — and shows you exactly what you should be earning.

**Effortless clocking**
⏱️ One-tap clock in and out, breaks, and reminders if you forget
🧩 Home and Lock Screen widgets, Live Activity and Dynamic Island with a live timer and a growing amount
⌚ An Apple Watch app
🎙️ Siri and Shortcuts — "Clock in with HoursTracker"
🌙 Every shift gets a name — morning, evening or night. Night follows the law: at least 2 hours between 22:00 and 06:00

**Pay that understands the law**
💵 Regular hours, 125%, 150%, rest days and holidays, travel allowance, breaks and the work week
⭐ A built-in holiday calendar you can edit: add, remove or move dates for each workplace
🏢 Contractor or company employee: mark when the company took you on — new rate and travel allowance, and pension and severance from that day, without changing earlier months
🧾 A breakdown of income tax, National Insurance and health tax from your credit points, marital status and age — net and gross
🏭 Several workplaces, each with its own rules

**Order and insight**
📊 Weekly and monthly History with daily amounts, totals and reports
📸 A smart scanner for payslips and timesheets, and a tidy payslip library
📤 Export to PDF and CSV
🤖 An optional smart assistant that answers questions about your hours and pay
💬 Chat with the developer from inside the app

**Your privacy**
🔒 Your data stays with you. Lock with Face ID, back up to an account or iCloud, and delete everything at any time
🌍 Hebrew, Arabic, English and Russian

Pay and tax figures are planning estimates only and are not legal, tax or accounting advice. Your payslip and the law decide your real pay.
